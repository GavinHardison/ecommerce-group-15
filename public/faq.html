Frequently Asked Questions

What is the return policy?

We offer a 30-day return policy on all items. Please ensure that the items are in their original condition.

How long does shipping take?

Shipping times vary based on your location, but typically it takes 5-7 business days for standard shipping.

Do you ship internationally?

Yes, we ship to most countries worldwide. Shipping fees and times may vary based on the destination.

How can I track my order?

Once your order has been shipped, you will receive an email with a tracking number and a link to track your package.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit cards, PayPal, and Apple Pay.
