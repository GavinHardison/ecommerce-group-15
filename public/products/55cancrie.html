55 Cancri e

55 Cancri e is a super-Earth that orbits incredibly close to its star, completing a full year in less than 18 hours. Its surface is thought to be an extreme lava world, with temperatures hot enough to melt rock and possibly oceans of molten magma. The planet may also have a thick, mineral-rich atmosphere filled with vaporized rock and exotic chemicals. With its intense heat, tidal forces, and possible day/night contrasts, 55 Cancri e is one of the most hostile—and scientifically fascinating—rocky exoplanets discovered.
