Jupiter

Jupiter is the fifth planet from the Sun and the largest planet in the Solar System. It is a gas giant primarily composed of hydrogen and helium, with a strong magnetic field and at least 79 moons. Jupiter is known for its prominent bands of clouds and the Great Red Spot, a massive storm that has been observed for centuries. Its immense size and gravitational influence have a significant impact on the orbits of other objects in the Solar System.
