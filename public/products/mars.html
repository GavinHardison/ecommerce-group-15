Mars

Mars, the red planet, is a cold desert world coated in iron oxide dust that gives it its distinct color. Its thin atmosphere—mostly carbon dioxide—cannot retain much heat, resulting in frigid temperatures. Mars features some of the Solar System's most impressive landscapes: Olympus Mons, the largest known volcano; Valles Marineris, a canyon system that's deeper and longer than the Grand Canyon; and ancient riverbeds that suggest liquid water once flowed across the surface. Scientists are intensely interested in Mars because its past conditions may have been suitable for life, and future missions aim to explore the possibility of human settlement.
