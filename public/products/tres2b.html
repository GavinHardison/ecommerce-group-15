TrES-2 b

TrES-2 b is a hot Jupiter famous for being the darkest known planet, reflecting almost no light—less than coal or black acrylic paint. Orbiting extremely close to its star, it is superheated, with an atmosphere too hot and turbulent for reflective clouds to form. The planet is likely filled with light-absorbing chemicals that keep it nearly pitch black, glowing only faintly with the red heat of its own temperature. Its unusual darkness makes TrES-2 b one of the most mysterious and visually striking exoplanets discovered.
