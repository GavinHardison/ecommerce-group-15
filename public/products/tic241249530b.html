TIC 241249530 b

TIC 241249530 b is an exoplanet discovered by NASA's TESS mission. It's classified as a hot Jupiter—a gas giant orbiting extremely close to its star, completing an orbit in just a few days. This tight orbit exposes the planet to intense heat and stellar radiation, likely inflating its atmosphere and causing powerful winds and temperature gradients. While much about this world remains unknown due to its distance and limited observational data, its size and proximity to its star make it an interesting target for atmospheric studies as telescope technology improves.
