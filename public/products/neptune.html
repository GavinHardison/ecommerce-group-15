Neptune

Neptune, the outermost major planet, is a deep blue ice giant known for its violent weather and extremely fast winds, which can exceed 1,200 mph. Its atmosphere contains hydrogen, helium, methane, and exotic clouds that form dramatic storms. Neptune also hosts Triton, a large moon that orbits backward—opposite its planet's rotation—suggesting it was captured from the Kuiper Belt. Triton has geysers and may be geologically active. The planet's great distance from the Sun makes it cold and dim, yet its dynamic atmosphere shows surprising complexity.
