Neptune

Neptune is the eighth and farthest known planet from the Sun in our solar system. It is classified as an ice giant due to its composition, which includes heavier elements like water, ammonia, and methane ices. Neptune has a striking deep blue color, primarily caused by the absorption of red light by methane in its atmosphere. The planet has a dynamic atmosphere with the fastest winds recorded in the solar system, reaching speeds of up to 2,100 kilometers per hour (1,300 miles per hour). Neptune has a faint ring system and at least 14 known moons, with Triton being the largest and most notable for its retrograde orbit and geologic activity.
