WASP-107 b

WASP-107 b is a low-density gas giant, often described as a “puffy” planet, orbiting very close to its star. Despite being similar in size to Jupiter, it has only a fraction of Jupiter's mass, giving it an extremely light and inflated atmosphere. This atmosphere is so extended that it is slowly escaping into space, forming a faint cloud around the planet. WASP-107 b's unusual density and atmospheric loss make it an important target for studying how gas giants evolve and how stellar radiation can strip away planetary envelopes.
