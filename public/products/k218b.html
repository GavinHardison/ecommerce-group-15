K2-18 b

K2-18 b is a super-Earth orbiting within the habitable zone of its star, where temperatures could allow liquid water to exist. It is larger and more massive than Earth, with a thick atmosphere containing hydrogen, helium, and hints of water vapor. While its surface conditions are still uncertain—possibly rocky, icy, or ocean-covered—K2-18 b is one of the most promising exoplanets for studying potential habitability beyond the Solar System.
