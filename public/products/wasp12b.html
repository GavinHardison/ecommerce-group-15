WASP-12b

WASP-12 b is an extremely hot Jupiter that orbits so close to its star that its “year” lasts just over a day. The intense heat causes the planet to balloon in size and darken to almost pitch black, absorbing nearly all light that hits it. Its atmosphere is being slowly pulled away by the star's gravity, forming a trail of escaping gas. Because it's literally being stretched and evaporated, WASP-12 b is considered one of the most distorted and short-lived planets known, offering a dramatic example of how extreme stellar environments can destroy a world.
