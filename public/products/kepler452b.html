Kepler-452 b

Kepler-452 b is a super-Earth orbiting within the habitable zone of a Sun-like star, often called “Earth's cousin.” It is about 60% larger than Earth and may have a rocky surface, though its exact composition is still unknown. The planet receives a similar amount of stellar energy as Earth, suggesting temperatures that could allow liquid water. While much remains uncertain, Kepler-452 b is considered one of the most promising candidates for studying potentially habitable worlds beyond our Solar System.
