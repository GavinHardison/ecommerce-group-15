Saturn

Saturn is the sixth planet from the Sun and the second-largest in our solar system, known for its stunning ring system composed of ice and rock particles. It is a gas giant primarily made of hydrogen and helium, with a thick atmosphere that features strong winds and storms. Saturn has at least 83 moons, including Titan, the second-largest moon in the solar system, which has a dense atmosphere and liquid hydrocarbon lakes. The planet's low density allows it to float in water, making it unique among the planets in our solar system.
